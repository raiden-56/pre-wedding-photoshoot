Our process

From first hello to forever

Six unhurried steps, designed so you can simply enjoy each other while we look after everything else.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Wander through our stories and find the light, the mood and the pace that feels like the two of you.

  2. 02

    Connect

    A long, unhurried call. We listen to how you met, what you love, and what you never want to forget.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Together we choose the location, the hour of light, outfits and the collection that fits your celebration.

  4. 04

    Capture

    No stiff poses. We guide gently and let real moments unfold — the laughter in between is where the magic lives.

  5. 05

    Curate

    Your private gallery arrives within weeks. Favourite, comment and choose the frames for your album together.

  6. 06

    Relive

    Share with family through a link or QR code, play the cinematic slideshow, and keep it all for years to come.
