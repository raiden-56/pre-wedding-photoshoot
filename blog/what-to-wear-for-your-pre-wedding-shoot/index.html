What to wear for your pre-wedding shoot

Colour palettes that photograph beautifully, and the fabrics that move with the wind.

Choose two or three looks that tell a story — one relaxed, one dressed up, one that feels like home.

Colours Earthy neutrals, dusty pastels and deep jewel tones all photograph beautifully. Avoid large logos and very fine stripes.

Movement Flowing fabrics like chiffon and organza catch the wind and add life to a frame.

Coordinate, don't match Complementary tones look more natural than identical colours.
